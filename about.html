What Is Web64?

Web64 is an experimental native web browser built for Nintendo 64 with libdragon.

The console fetches pages, parses HTML64 and CSS64, lays out their contents, and renders the result itself.

The Desktop Modem

The web64-bridge desktop program acts as a modem.

Its web64-net executable creates a TAP interface, provides routing and NAT, and carries Ethernet frames over the cartridge USB connection.
